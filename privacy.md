# MeiPDF 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-08-27

---

## 简体中文

**MeiPDF 不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人数据。**

- 你打开的文档、标注、书签、阅读进度与偏好设置，仅保存在你的 Mac 本地（应用自身的存储容器内），开发者无法访问。
- 应用不含任何广告、分析或第三方追踪组件；除系统框架外，应用不发起任何网络请求。
- 如果你通过 Mac App Store 购买 MeiPDF，整个支付流程由 Apple 处理，开发者不会收到你的任何支付或账户信息。
- 文件访问说明：应用只在你通过「打开 / 导出 / 打印」面板主动选择文件，或将文件拖入窗口、在 Finder 中双击时访问该文件，不会访问你的其它文件。
- 如对本政策有疑问，请通过仓库 Issues 联系：<https://github.com/mei-pub/MeiPDF/issues>

## English

**MeiPDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- The documents you open, your annotations, bookmarks, reading progress, and preferences are stored only on your Mac (inside the app's own storage container). The developer has no access to them.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking components. Apart from system frameworks, the app makes no network requests.
- If you purchase MeiPDF from the Mac App Store, the entire payment is processed by Apple. The developer never receives any payment or account information from you.
- File access: the app only touches a file when you explicitly choose it via an Open / Export / Print panel, drag it into the window, or double-click it in Finder. It never accesses any other files.
- Questions about this policy: <https://github.com/mei-pub/MeiPDF/issues>
