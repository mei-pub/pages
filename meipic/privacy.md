# MeiPic 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-08-28

---

## 简体中文

**MeiPic 不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人数据。**

- 你浏览的图片文件夹、星级标记与偏好设置，仅保存在你的 Mac 本地（应用自身的存储容器内），开发者无法访问。
- 应用不含任何广告、分析或第三方追踪组件；除系统框架外，应用不发起任何网络请求。
- 如果你通过 Mac App Store 获取 MeiPic，整个流程由 Apple 处理，开发者不会收到你的任何支付或账户信息。
- 文件访问说明：应用只在你通过「打开」面板主动选择文件夹、或将文件夹拖入窗口 / Dock 图标时，只读访问该文件夹内的图片；应用不会修改、移动或删除你的任何照片文件（零侵入）。
- 如对本政策有疑问，请通过仓库 Issues 联系：<https://github.com/mei-pub/pages/issues>

## English

**MeiPic does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- The folders you browse, star ratings, and preferences are stored only on your Mac (inside the app's own storage container). The developer has no access to them.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking components. Apart from system frameworks, the app makes no network requests.
- If you get MeiPic from the Mac App Store, the entire purchase flow is processed by Apple. The developer never receives any payment or account information from you.
- File access: the app only reads images in folders you explicitly choose via the Open panel or drag onto the window / Dock icon. It never modifies, moves, or deletes any of your photo files.
- Questions about this policy: <https://github.com/mei-pub/pages/issues>
